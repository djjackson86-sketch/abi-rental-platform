# Jackapp and Sano Trailers — Client Information Notice

**Version 1.1 · 2026-10-11**

This notice explains the basic responsibilities between **Jackapp** and **Sano Trailers** regarding the customer information stored and processed in the Sano Trailers rental system.

## 1. Parties

**Client / Business: Sano Trailers**
Sano Trailers owns and manages its customer information and decides how it is used.

**Service provider: Jackapp**
Jackapp provides, maintains and supports the software system Sano Trailers uses.

## 2. Sano Trailers' responsibilities

Sano Trailers is responsible for:

- collecting customer information lawfully;
- telling customers why their information is needed;
- making sure customer information entered into the system is accurate;
- deciding how long customer information should be kept;
- responding to customer requests about their personal information;
- only using customer information for proper business purposes, such as rentals, bookings, invoices, payments, repairs, sales and customer communication.

## 3. Jackapp's responsibilities

Jackapp is responsible for:

- hosting, maintaining and supporting the software system;
- only accessing Sano Trailers customer information when needed for support, maintenance, troubleshooting, development, backups or security purposes;
- not selling or sharing Sano Trailers customer information for marketing or unrelated purposes;
- keeping access to the system restricted to authorised support personnel;
- helping Sano Trailers keep the system secure and operational.

## 4. Customer information processed

The system may store information such as:

- customer names;
- phone numbers;
- email addresses;
- physical addresses;
- rental, booking, sales, repair and invoice information;
- payment records;
- notes needed to manage customer orders or services.

## 5. How Jackapp keeps information secure

Jackapp uses reasonable technical and operational measures to help protect the information in the system, including:

- password-protected user accounts;
- restricted admin access;
- secure hosting and database services;
- access controls so staff only see what they are allowed to see;
- backups where needed for recovery;
- support access only when required;
- keeping system credentials and technical access details confidential;
- monitoring and fixing security issues where identified.

## 6. Access to customer information

Jackapp will only access Sano Trailers customer information when it is necessary to:

- fix a technical issue;
- provide support requested by Sano Trailers;
- maintain or improve the system;
- investigate an error;
- restore or protect system data;
- comply with a lawful instruction from Sano Trailers.

Jackapp will not use Sano Trailers customer information for its own marketing or unrelated business purposes.

## 7. Sharing of information

Jackapp may use trusted service providers needed to run the system, such as hosting, database, email, messaging or backup providers. These providers are used only where necessary for the system to function.

## 8. Security incidents

If Jackapp has reasonable grounds to believe that Sano Trailers customer information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, Jackapp will tell Sano Trailers immediately, and will assist with investigating and resolving the issue.

## 9. Ownership of information

Customer information remains the information of **Sano Trailers**. Jackapp does not claim ownership of Sano Trailers customer information.

## 10. Ending the service

If the service ends, Jackapp will reasonably assist Sano Trailers with access to its information, export of data, or deletion of information where appropriate and technically possible.

## 11. Keeping this record

Sano can keep this notice as a record of how Jackapp handles and helps protect its customer information. There is no signature form to complete for this record.

POPIA requires a written contract covering the security measures used by an operator. It does not expressly require handwritten signatures for that contract. Those terms can be included in the existing written service agreement, or agreed through clear written confirmation, such as an email exchange between authorised representatives of Sano and Jackapp. The agreed terms should require appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect the information.

Keep this notice together with the relevant service agreement or written confirmation. Downloading or filing this notice alone does not establish that both parties have agreed to the terms, and this notice does not certify full POPIA compliance.
