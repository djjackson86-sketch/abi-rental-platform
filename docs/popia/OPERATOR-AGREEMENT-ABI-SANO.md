## 1. Background and status of the parties

1.1 Sano Trailers conducts a trailer hire, sales and service business and, in doing so, processes personal
information of its customers and other individuals.

1.2 Sano Trailers determines the purpose of and the means for that processing and is accordingly the
**responsible party** as defined in POPIA.

1.3 The Operator provides, hosts and maintains a rental management software platform (the "**Platform**") for
Sano Trailers, and in doing so processes personal information **on behalf of and on the instruction of**
Sano Trailers. The Operator is accordingly an **operator** as defined in POPIA.

1.4 POPIA section 21(1) requires a responsible party to ensure, in terms of a written contract, that an operator
establishes and maintains the security measures contemplated in section 19. This Agreement is that contract.

1.5 The parties record that the Operator **is not** the responsible party's information officer, and does not
determine the purposes of the processing. The Operator may not process the personal information for its own
purposes, and may not sell, rent or trade it.

## 3. Scope, purpose and duration of processing

3.1 **Purpose.** The Operator processes Personal Information only to provide, operate, maintain, support and
secure the Platform for Sano Trailers, and for no other purpose.

3.2 **Nature and categories** of Personal Information, categories of data subjects, and the operations
performed are set out in **Schedule A**.

3.3 **Duration.** The Operator processes Personal Information for the duration of the parties' service
relationship, and thereafter only as permitted by clause 10 (Return and deletion).

3.4 **No independent decision-making.** The Operator does not decide who the data subjects are, what is
collected, why it is collected, how long it is kept, or to whom it is disclosed. Those decisions belong to
Sano Trailers. Where the Operator believes an instruction would breach POPIA or any other law, it must tell
Sano Trailers immediately, in writing, and may suspend compliance with that instruction until it is confirmed
in writing.

## 4. Operator obligations

The Operator must:

4.1 **Process only on instruction.** Process Personal Information only on the Instructions of Sano Trailers,
and only for the purposes in clause 3.1.

4.2 **Confidentiality.** Treat all Personal Information as confidential, and ensure that every person who
processes it — employees, contractors and sub-processors — is subject to a written duty of confidentiality that
survives the end of their engagement.

4.3 **Security.** Establish and maintain the security measures required by POPIA section 19, being appropriate,
reasonable technical and organisational measures to prevent loss of, damage to, or unauthorised destruction of
Personal Information, and unlawful access to or processing of it. The measures in place at signature are set out
in **Schedule B**, and may not be materially reduced during the term without Sano Trailers' written agreement.

4.4 **Notify of compromises immediately.** Notify Sano Trailers **immediately** upon becoming aware of any
Incident, and in any event within **[24] hours**. The notification must include, to the extent known: what
happened, when, what categories of Personal Information and how many data subjects are affected, the likely
consequences, the measures taken or proposed to contain and remedy it, and the Operator's point of contact.
The Operator must keep Sano Trailers informed of developments and must not notify any third party (including the
Information Regulator or affected data subjects) about the Incident **on Sano Trailers' behalf** unless Sano
Trailers instructs it to in writing. *(The duty to notify the Information Regulator and data subjects under
POPIA section 22 rests on Sano Trailers.)*

4.5 **Assist with data-subject rights.** Assist Sano Trailers, at Sano Trailers' cost where the request is
unusually onerous, with any request by a data subject to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of
their Personal Information (POPIA sections 23, 24 and 11(3)), within **[5] business days** of being asked, and
in any event so as to allow Sano Trailers to meet its own obligations.

4.6 **Assist with compliance.** Provide reasonable assistance to Sano Trailers with its POPIA obligations,
including its security obligations, its notification duties, and any personal information impact assessment or
consultation with the Information Regulator.

4.7 **Records and audit.** Keep records of the processing carried out on behalf of Sano Trailers (including
system logs, access records and any Incidents) for at least **[12] months**, and make them available to Sano
Trailers on reasonable written request. Sano Trailers may, on **[14] days'** written notice and no more than
**[once per year]** (or more often following an Incident), audit the Operator's compliance with this Agreement.
The audit must not compromise the security of other clients' data, and the Operator may require confidentiality
undertakings and a reasonable scope.

4.8 **Accurate and up to date.** Take reasonable steps to keep Personal Information accurate, and correct or
delete it on Sano Trailers' instruction, subject to clause 6.

4.9 **Staff.** Limit access to Personal Information to those personnel who need it to perform the service,
grant access through individually identifiable accounts (no shared logins), and revoke access promptly when a
person leaves or changes role.

4.10 **Data minimisation.** Not copy Personal Information into any environment, device, tool or service that is
not needed to provide the Platform, and not use live customer data for development or testing where
de-identified or synthetic data would do.

4.11 **Sub-processors.** Comply with clause 5.

4.12 **Return and delete.** Comply with clause 10 on termination.

7.1 The Operator must not sell, rent, trade, publish or otherwise make Personal Information available to any

## 9. Liability

9.1 The Operator is liable to Sano Trailers for loss, damage or expense caused by the Operator's breach of this
Agreement, including any fine, penalty or compensation Sano Trailers is ordered to pay to the extent caused by
that breach.

9.2 The parties acknowledge that POPIA section 21(1) places the duty to secure this Agreement on Sano Trailers
and does not transfer Sano Trailers' own accountability to the Operator. Nothing in this Agreement relieves Sano
Trailers of its POPIA obligations.

9.3 **Limit of liability.** **[RECOMMENDED WORDING — confirm before signature]**

  (a) Jackapp's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in
      contract, delict or otherwise, is limited to **the total fees actually paid by Sano Trailers to Jackapp in
      the 12 months immediately preceding the date on which the claim first arose**.

  (b) The limit in (a) does **not** apply to liability arising from Jackapp's **wilful misconduct or gross
      negligence**, which remains unlimited.

  (c) Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, or loss of business,
      except to the extent that a fine, administrative penalty or compensation order imposed on Sano Trailers
      by the Information Regulator is a direct result of Jackapp's breach of this Agreement.

  (d) Each claim is separate, but the aggregate of all claims remains subject to the limit in (a).

*Why this shape:* a sole proprietorship has no separate legal personality, so Donovan Jackson is personally
liable — the cap is the only thing standing between a system failure and his personal assets. Carving out only
wilful misconduct and gross negligence keeps the cap commercially realistic while not protecting genuinely bad
behaviour. **Data-protection breaches are deliberately *not* carved out**, so a breach claim is capped; if Sano
Trailers insists on an uncapped data-breach exposure, consider capping it at a higher multiple (e.g. 2× the
12-month fees) rather than removing the cap entirely.

*Alternatives if the above is not agreed:* (i) a fixed rand cap, e.g. R250,000; (ii) a cap at the fees paid,
with **no** carve-outs at all (most protective of Jackapp); (iii) no cap (unlimited personal exposure —
not recommended for a sole proprietor).

9.4 **[Optional — TO CONFIRM]** Jackapp confirms that it holds, or will obtain within **[30] days** of
signature, professional indemnity / cyber liability insurance and will maintain it for the term, and will
provide proof of cover on written request.

## 10. Return and deletion on termination

10.1 On termination or expiry of the service relationship, the Operator must, at Sano Trailers' choice and
within **[30] days**:

  (a) return the Personal Information to Sano Trailers in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
      (a full data export); and/or

  (b) delete or destroy the Personal Information in a manner that prevents its reconstruction in an
      intelligible form (POPIA section 14(5)), and confirm in writing that it has done so.

10.2 The Operator may retain Personal Information beyond termination only where a law requires it, and only for
as long as that law requires, or where the parties agree in writing. Any retained information remains subject
to clauses 4, 7 and 8.

10.3 Personal Information in backups ages out on the Operator's normal backup rotation, and any restoration of
a backup must be followed by re-application of the deletions.

## Schedule B — Security measures in place at signature

| Area | Measure |
|---|---|
| Access control | Individually identifiable accounts per person; no shared logins; role-based permissions; restricted staff accounts see only their permitted modules |
| Branch scoping | Branch-limited staff can only see their own branch's customers, orders and stock |

**Cross-border position — two different destinations:**

- **Database (Ireland, European Union):** the GDPR provides a level of protection substantially similar to
  POPIA, which supports section 72(a) for that transfer.
- **Application host (Oregon, United States):** the United States does **not** have a general adequacy finding
  equivalent to the EU's. This transfer therefore relies on section 72(a) by way of the **binding agreement**
  route — Render's and AWS's data-processing terms, read with this Agreement, impose GDPR-equivalent
  protections — and/or on section 72(c) (necessary for the performance of the contract with the data subject,
  since the Platform cannot process the rental without processing the data).

**Recommendation for a cleaner position:** Render supports an **EU (Frankfurt)** region. Recreating the
application service in Frankfurt would place all processing in the EU, leaving only the (adequately protected)
EU destination and removing the US transfer question entirely. This should be decided before the Privacy Notice
is finalised, because the Notice must state the destination and the level of protection accurately.
