# Review: Sano–Jackapp client information notice and signatures

## Conclusion

Keep the downloadable document as a plain-English client information record, without a signature table. Do not describe downloading or holding the record as completing the required operator contract or as proving POPIA compliance.

POPIA section 21(1) expressly requires a **written contract** between responsible party and operator covering the establishment and maintenance of section 19 security measures. It does not expressly prescribe signatures. A unilateral information notice is not, by itself, proof of an agreed contract.

ECTA section 12 allows the writing requirement to be met by a data message accessible for later reference. Sections 13(5) and 22 support electronic expressions of agreement and electronic contract formation; absence of a handwritten signature does not by itself invalidate an electronic agreement. Actual agreement, authorised representatives, identifiable terms and retained evidence still matter. If another applicable law or the parties' existing terms require a signature or a specific amendment process, honour those requirements.

A clear retained email exchange accepting identified operator/security terms may evidence an agreement. An existing written service contract may already cover these terms, but it must be inspected before treating the requirement as satisfied. Merely acknowledging receipt is not necessarily acceptance of terms.

## Changes made

- Label the Settings download a client information notice, not a sign-required agreement.
- Remove the blank signature/acceptance table and the assertion that the PDF itself becomes the arrangement on acceptance.
- Explain keeping the notice alongside the relevant existing service agreement or written confirmation.
- Preserve the existing security/access/incident responsibilities and the immediate incident-notification wording.
- Preserve the old download URL as an alias; add the canonical information-notice URL.
- Leave customer privacy publications, customer consents, officer registration status and all existing business records untouched.
