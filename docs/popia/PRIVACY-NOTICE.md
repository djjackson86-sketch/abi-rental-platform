## 2. What personal information we collect

We collect only what we need to hire out, sell, service and recover trailers.

**When you are a customer / hirer:**

| Category | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Identity & contact | Name, email address, telephone number |
| Address | Street address, suburb, city, province, postal code |
| Verification | Whether your details were verified, and a copy of your identity document or driver's licence **only where you provide it for verification** |
| Vehicle details | The make, colour and registration number of the vehicle that will tow the trailer (where applicable and voluntarily provided) |
| Alternative contact | The name and number of a person we may contact if we cannot reach you |
| Business details | Company name, VAT number and company registration number (business customers) |
| Transaction | Orders, quotations, invoices, rental periods, deposits, payments, outstanding balances, damage and return records |
| Communications | Emails, WhatsApp messages and notes from calls with us |

**When you use our online store or booking system:** the information you type into a form, plus standard
technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) needed to run and secure the site.

**We do not knowingly collect the personal information of children** (under 18) without the consent of a
competent person. Our rentals are not aimed at children.

**Special personal information:** we do not intentionally collect race, health, religious, political, trade-union,
biometric, criminal or sexual information. If you volunteer something of that kind in a note, we will not use it.

## 3. Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis (POPIA s11) |
|---|---|
| Quoting, booking, hiring out, returning and servicing trailers | Performance of the contract with you |
| Invoicing, taking payment, recovering deposits and outstanding money | Contract + our legitimate interest in being paid |
| Verifying who you are before handing over an asset worth tens of thousands of rand | Legitimate interest + our contractual right to protect our property |
| Contacting your alternative contact if we cannot reach you (e.g. overdue return) | Legitimate interest |
| Keeping records we are required by law to keep (tax, company, consumer) | Legal obligation |
| Recovering our property, defending or pursuing claims, insurance claims | Legitimate interest |
| Sending you marketing about our products and services | **Your consent** — see section 8 |
| Keeping the website secure and preventing fraud or abuse | Legitimate interest |

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making that has legal
consequences for you.

**Supply of your information is voluntary** — but if you do not give us the details we need, we may not be able
to hire a trailer to you, invoice you, or pay out or refund a deposit. Where we are required by law to collect
something, we will say so at the time.

## 4. Who else receives your information

We share personal information only with the following **categories of recipients**, and only as far as needed:

- **Our service providers ("operators")** who process it on our behalf under written instruction — these include our hosting provider, our database provider, our email provider, our IT support, and our accounting service.
- **Payment and banking providers**, to process payments and refunds.
- **Insurers and assessors**, if a trailer is damaged or a claim arises.
- **Debt collectors, tracing agents and attorneys**, if money is owed to us or our property is not returned.
- **Law enforcement, courts, the Information Regulator, SARS and other regulators**, where the law requires or permits it.
- **Your alternative contact**, if we cannot reach you about a trailer that is due back.

We require operators to keep your information confidential and secure, to use it only for the purpose we gave
them, and to have reasonable security measures in place (POPIA sections 20 and 21).

## 5. Sending your information outside South Africa

Our booking system and database are hosted on cloud infrastructure located **outside South Africa**:

- our **database** is hosted in the **European Union (Ireland)**; and
- our **application** is hosted in the **United States (Oregon)**.

POPIA section 72 allows this where the recipient is bound by a law, binding corporate rules or a **binding
agreement** that provides a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where the transfer is
necessary to perform our contract with you. The European Union's data protection law (the GDPR) is recognised
as providing that level of protection. For the United States, we rely on the **contractual data-protection terms
imposed on our providers** (including our operator agreement with our service provider), which require your
information to be kept secure, used only on our instruction, and protected to a standard equivalent to POPIA —
together with the fact that the transfer is necessary for us to provide the hire service you have asked for.

Your information is **not** transferred to any country that does not protect it to this standard.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and as long as the law
requires. Our full **Retention Policy and Schedule** is available on request, and sets out the period for each
record type with the reason for it.

In short:

- **Tax, VAT and accounting records** — at least **5 years** from the date of the relevant return (Tax Administration Act), and **7 years** for company records (Companies Act).
- **Rental agreements, damage and deposit records** — **7 years**, because they are the evidence we need under the Companies Act and for potential claims.
- **Customer contact details** — kept while you are an active customer and for a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted or de-identified.
- **Identity document / licence copies taken for verification** — deleted once verification is done and the rental is closed and settled, unless a claim or dispute is live.
- **Marketing consents and opt-outs** — kept as long as needed to honour your choice; an opt-out is kept indefinitely so we do not contact you again by mistake.
- **Website enquiries that never became a customer** — **12 months**.
- **CCTV footage** (if a branch has cameras) — a short rolling window, kept longer only if an incident makes a specific clip evidence.
- **Staff / employment records** — per the Basic Conditions of Employment Act and tax law.

When a retention period ends we delete or destroy the record in a way that prevents it being reconstructed,
or we de-identify it so it can no longer be linked to you.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

1. **Be told** that we hold your information and what we do with it (this notice).
2. **Access** it — ask us for a record of the personal information we hold about you (POPIA section 23).
3. **Correct or delete** information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading, or that we obtained unlawfully — or that we are no longer allowed to keep (section 24).
4. **Object** to our processing of your information, on reasonable grounds (section 11(3)).
5. **Opt out of marketing** at any time (section 69).
6. **Complain** to the Information Regulator.

**How to use them:** email **info@sanotrailers.co.za** with the subject *Privacy Request*, or deliver a written
request to 229 Summit Road, Midrand. We will ask you to prove who you are first (so we do not hand your
information to somebody else), and we will respond within a reasonable time.

There is a small fee for an access request where the law allows one; we will tell you before charging it.

**We may keep information even after a deletion request** where the law requires us to keep it (for example tax
and company records), or where we reasonably need it as evidence for a claim that is still possible. If we do
that, we will tell you why.

## 8. Direct marketing

We will only send you marketing by email, SMS or WhatsApp if:

- you are an existing customer and we are telling you about our own similar products and services, **and** you were given the chance to opt out when we collected your details; or
- you have specifically opted in; or
- you are not an existing customer and you have specifically consented (POPIA section 69).

Every marketing message we send includes a way to opt out, and we honour opt-outs.

## 9. How we keep it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including password-protected
access, staff accounts limited to what each person needs for their job, separate access by branch, secure
connections, and backups. Only staff who need your information to do their work can see it.

**If there is a security compromise** (a data breach), we will notify the Information Regulator and any affected
people as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA section 22 requires.
