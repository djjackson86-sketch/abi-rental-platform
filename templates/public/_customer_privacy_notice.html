What we collect and why

We collect the contact, address, business, tow-vehicle and verification information needed to handle enquiries, arrange rentals, verify customers, process payments and refunds, and manage returns, damage, overdue trailers and outstanding amounts. We also keep relevant booking, payment and communication records, and customer-account information such as discounts, credit arrangements and restrictions.

We usually collect this from you. If you supply an alternative contact, we collect their name, telephone number and relationship to you from you, to contact them if we cannot reach you about your rental. Please let them know and share this notice with them.

Your choices

You choose whether to provide your information. Details needed for a booking, customer verification or payment are required for that service; without them we may be unable to proceed. Other information and marketing choices are optional. Tax and accounting record obligations arise under applicable tax legislation, including the Tax Administration Act, and company-record obligations under the Companies Act where applicable.

Who receives it

We share relevant information only as needed with authorised staff, our booking and IT service providers, accountants, payment providers, insurers, recovery agents and legal advisers, and authorities where legally required or permitted.

Overseas processing

The production cloud booking service uses database hosting in Ireland (European Union) and application hosting in Oregon, United States. EU data-protection law protects information processed in Ireland; US protection depends on applicable provider contracts rather than an equivalent general privacy law. Overseas recipients must provide safeguards meeting POPIA, including protection for onward transfers.

Keeping your information

We use reasonable safeguards and keep information only for as long as needed for the stated purposes, applicable legal obligations or unresolved claims. We then securely delete it or remove identifying details.

Marketing

Marketing is optional and separate from your registration or booking. We send electronic marketing only where POPIA permits it, and you can opt out at any time.

Your rights

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to object to processing where POPIA permits. Some records must be retained for legal reasons.
