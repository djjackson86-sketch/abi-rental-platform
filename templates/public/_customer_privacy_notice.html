{{ settings.company_name or 'Sano Trailers' }} is the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). We decide why your information is collected and how it is used.

Address: 229 Summit Road, Midrand. Privacy enquiries: info@sanotrailers.co.za · 010 221 1723.

Who collects your information?

{{ settings.company_name or 'Sano Trailers' }} collects and controls your personal information. Our booking, hosting and database providers process it for us only on our instructions, so they are our operators rather than the people who decide how it is used. Any privacy question or request goes to us, using the details above with the subject line Privacy Request.

What information is collected?

We collect only what we need to hire out, service and recover trailers:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number and address.
  • Business details — company name, VAT number and registration number — where you are invoiced as a business.
  • The make, colour, registration number, VIN and engine number of the vehicle that will tow the trailer. You give us these, or we read them from the vehicle’s licence disc.
  • Whether your details were verified, and a copy of your identity document or driver’s licence only where you provide it for verification.
  • The name, telephone number and relationship of an alternative contact you give us.
  • Your rental records — quotes, bookings, invoices, payments, deposits, outstanding amounts, and damage or return notes — and account information such as discounts, credit arrangements and restrictions.

We usually collect this from you. If you give us an alternative contact, we collect their details from you so we can reach them about your rental; please let them know and share this notice with them.

Why is it collected?

  • To process and confirm your booking and to hire out, return and service trailers.
  • To record which vehicle is towing which trailer, using the details on the vehicle’s licence disc.
  • To contact you about your rental, including a trailer that is due back.
  • To generate invoices, take payment, and recover deposits and outstanding amounts.
  • To verify who you are before we hand over a trailer.
  • To keep the records the law requires us to keep.

You choose whether to give us your information. The details we need for a booking, verification or payment are required for that service; without them we may not be able to proceed. Other information is optional. Some records must be kept under law — tax and accounting records under the Tax Administration Act, and company records under the Companies Act.

Who has access to your information?

We share your information only as far as it is needed, with:

  • our own authorised staff;
  • our booking, hosting, database and IT service providers, who process it for us on our instructions;
  • our accountants and auditors;
  • payment and banking providers;
  • insurers and assessors;
  • debt collectors, tracing agents and attorneys, where money is owed to us or a trailer is not returned; and
  • the authorities — including SARS and the Information Regulator — where the law requires or permits it.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long is it kept?

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and as long as the law requires or a claim is still possible. Tax and accounting records are kept for the periods set by the Tax Administration Act, and company and rental records for the periods set by the Companies Act. When that period ends we securely delete the information or remove the details that identify you. A legal hold — a tax audit, a dispute or a regulator’s request — may require us to keep a record longer; we release it when the matter ends.

How is it secured?

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information: password-protected access, staff accounts limited to what each person needs for their job, separate access by branch, secure connections and backups. If there is a security compromise, we notify the Information Regulator and the affected people as POPIA requires.

Where is it processed?

Our booking service uses cloud database hosting in Ireland (European Union) and application hosting in Oregon, United States. EU data-protection law protects information processed in Ireland; protection in the United States depends on the contractual data-protection terms our providers are bound by, rather than an equivalent general privacy law. Overseas recipients must provide safeguards that meet POPIA, including protection for onward transfers.

Marketing

Marketing is optional and separate from your booking or registration. We send electronic marketing only where POPIA permits it, and you can opt out at any time.

Your rights under POPIA

You may ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or delete information that is wrong, or that we may no longer keep;
  • stop processing your information, on reasonable grounds; and
  • stop sending you marketing.

Some records must be kept for legal reasons, and we will tell you why if that applies. To use any of these rights, contact info@sanotrailers.co.za with the subject line Privacy Request.

Information Regulator

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za · 010 023 5200 · inforegulator.org.za. You can complain to the Regulator without coming to us first, but we would appreciate the chance to fix it.
