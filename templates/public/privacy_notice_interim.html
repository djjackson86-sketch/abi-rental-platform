What we do with the information you give us

We collect only what we need to quote, hire out, return and service trailers: your name, contact details, address and — for a rental — the details of the vehicle that will tow the trailer. We keep transaction records (orders, invoices, deposits, payments) and we keep them only as long as the law and our own retention policy require. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights, and how to complain

You may ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to our processing of it, opt out of marketing at any time, and complain to the Information Regulator. Until the full notice is published, send any such request to info@sanotrailers.co.za with the subject Privacy Request, or deliver it in writing to 229 Summit Road, Midrand.
