2. What personal information we collect, and where it comes from

We collect only what we need to hire out, sell, service and recover trailers.

Almost all of the personal information we hold is given to us by you — on a form at the branch, over the phone or WhatsApp, or through our online store, booking and registration pages. Where we receive information about you from somebody else (for example your alternative contact, an insurer or assessor handling a claim, a tracing agent, or a credit or verification service), we will tell you what we received and who gave it to us when you ask.

How we capture your vehicle details. You may give us your vehicle details on a form, or we may read them from the licence disc or licence document of the vehicle you bring to us — either by photographing the disc and reading its barcode, or by typing what is printed on it. A vehicle's registration number, NaTIS registration number, VIN and engine number are personal information: they identify a vehicle, and through it a person. We use them only to record which vehicle is towing which trailer, to check that the vehicle is roadworthy and licensed for the hire, and — if a trailer is not returned — to trace it.

If you are an alternative contact (the person a customer asks us to ring if we cannot reach them): we hold your name and telephone number, given to us by that customer, and we use them only to contact you about a trailer that is due back. We will tell you what we hold about you and why, if you ask us to, or if we contact you. You may ask us to remove your details at any time.

4. Who else receives your information

We share personal information only with the following categories of recipients, and only as far as needed:

  • Our service providers ("operators") who process it on our behalf under written instruction — our hosting provider, our database provider, our email provider, our IT support and our accounting service.
  • Payment and banking providers, to process payments and refunds.
  • Insurers and assessors, if a trailer is damaged or a claim arises.
  • Debt collectors, tracing agents and attorneys, if money is owed to us or our property is not returned.
  • Security service providers, where a branch is monitored by cameras and an incident is investigated.
  • Law enforcement, courts, the Information Regulator, SARS and other regulators, where the law requires or permits it.
  • Your alternative contact, if we cannot reach you about a trailer that is due back.

We require operators to keep your information confidential and secure, to use it only for the purpose we gave them, and to have reasonable security measures in place (POPIA sections 20 and 21).

5. Sending your information outside South Africa

Our booking system and database are hosted on cloud infrastructure located outside South Africa:

  • our database is hosted in the European Union (Ireland); and
  • our application is hosted in the United States (Oregon).

POPIA section 72 allows this where the recipient is bound by a law, binding corporate rules or a binding agreement that provides a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where the transfer is necessary to perform our contract with you. The European Union's data protection law (the GDPR) is recognised as providing that level of protection. For the United States we rely on the contractual data-protection terms imposed on our providers, together with the fact that the transfer is necessary to provide the hire service you asked for.

Your information is not transferred to any country that does not protect it to this standard.

6. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and as long as the law requires. Our full Retention Policy and Schedule is available on request and sets out the period for each record type with the reason for it. In short:

  • Tax, VAT and accounting records — at least 5 years from the date of the relevant return (Tax Administration Act), and 7 years for company records (Companies Act).
  • Rental agreements, vehicle records, damage and deposit records — 7 years, because they are the evidence we need under the Companies Act and for potential claims.
  • Customer contact details — kept while you are an active customer and for a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted or de-identified.
  • Identity document or licence copies taken for verification — deleted once verification is done and the rental is closed and settled, unless a claim or dispute is live.
  • Marketing consents and opt-outs — kept as long as needed to honour your choice; an opt-out is kept so we do not contact you again by mistake.
  • Website enquiries that never became a customer — 12 months.

When a retention period ends we delete or destroy the record so that it cannot be reconstructed or linked back to you, or we de-identify it. Where a backup copy is restored, we re-apply any deletions that happened after that backup was taken.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  1. Be told that we hold your information and what we do with it (this notice).
  2. Access it — ask us for a record of the personal information we hold about you (POPIA section 23).
  3. Correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading, or that we obtained unlawfully — or that we are no longer allowed to keep (section 24).
  4. Object to our processing of your information, on reasonable grounds (section 11(3)).
  5. Opt out of marketing at any time (section 69).
  6. Complain to the Information Regulator (section 74).

How to use them: email info@sanotrailers.co.za with the subject Privacy Request, or deliver a written request to 229 Summit Road, Midrand. We will ask you to prove who you are first, so that we do not hand your information to somebody else, and we will respond within a reasonable time. There is a small fee for an access request where the law allows one; we will tell you before charging it.

We may keep information even after a deletion request where the law requires us to keep it (for example tax and company records), or where we reasonably need it as evidence for a claim that is still possible. If we do that, we will tell you why.

8. Direct marketing

We will only send you marketing by email, SMS or WhatsApp if you are an existing customer and we are telling you about our own similar products and services, and you were given the chance to opt out when we collected your details; or you have specifically opted in; or you are not an existing customer and you have specifically consented (POPIA section 69). Every marketing message we send includes a way to opt out, and we honour opt-outs.

9. How we keep it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including password-protected access, staff accounts limited to what each person needs for their job, separate access by branch, secure connections and backups. Only staff who need your information to do their work can see it.

If there is a security compromise (a data breach), we will notify the Information Regulator and any affected people as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA section 22 requires.

10. Cookies and website analytics

Our website uses the minimum needed to run and secure the booking process. If we add analytics or tracking that identifies you, we will disclose it here and, where no other lawful basis applies, ask for your consent.
