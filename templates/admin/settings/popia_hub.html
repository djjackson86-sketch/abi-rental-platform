3. Register an Information Officer

Sano must register its Information Officer with South Africa's Information Regulator, as required by section 55(2) of POPIA. For a private business, the Information Officer is normally the head of the business.

Register Information OfficerOfficial guidance and forms

Register through the Regulator's eServices portal and keep the confirmation. Entering an officer's details in this app does not register them. If the portal is unavailable, the official guidance page provides the manual form and submission instructions.
