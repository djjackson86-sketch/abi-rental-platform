2 · Your Information Officer

The Information Officer is the person who answers POPIA questions from customers and from the Regulator. This is usually the owner or a manager.

Have you registered your Information Officer with the Information Regulator?

Not registered yet? Registration is free. Go to https://eservices.inforegulator.org.za. You will need your ID number and your business details. Save this step, register, then come back and tick “Yes”.

Why it matters: under s55(2), an Information Officer may only take up their duties once registered. Until yours is registered, the head of the business carries the POPIA duties personally — and the notice cannot be published while this reads “Not yet”.
